Term 1 Activities

Below is the planned focus for the first term of the academic year:

  • January: New Year Holiday, staff preparation and orientation, first term opening, induction for Form 1 and Form 5 learners, baseline assessments and timetable commencement.
  • February: Regular teaching and learning, continuous assessments, project/SBA monitoring, academic progress review, Robert Gabriel Mugabe National Youth Day holiday and parent/guardian engagement.
  • March: Mid and end-of-term revision, mock/assessment activities, learner progress evaluation, departmental academic review, end-of-term examinations, report preparation and the close of Term 1.
