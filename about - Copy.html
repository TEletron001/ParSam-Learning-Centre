Our School

About ParSam

A secondary learning community in Prospect Waterfalls, Harare, committed to academic excellence and the growth of every learner.

A place to learn and grow

Founded in 2026, ParSam Learning Centre is a private secondary school serving learners from Form 1 to Form 6. We aim to provide a safe, supportive and technologically integrated environment where students can develop academically and personally.

Our Vision

To inspire and educate, helping every child achieve their full potential and become a lifelong learner.

Our Mission

To empower students through quality education, fostering excellence in learning, critical thinking and personal growth.

Our Values

Integrity, excellence, innovation, compassion and resilience guide our school community.
